## Overview

This project is a practical exploration of reinforcement learning through implementations of core algorithms. The goal is to understand how agents learn to make sequential decisions by interacting with an environment and maximizing cumulative reward.

## Implemented algorithms

- **Dynamic Programming:** policy iteration and value iteration.
- **Tabular Reinforcement Learning:** Q-learning and SARSA.
- **Deep Reinforcement Learning:** Deep Q-Networks (DQN) and Double DQN.
- **Policy Gradient Methods:** REINFORCE.
- **Policy Optimization:** Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO).

## What I explored

The implementations focus on the relationship between value-based and policy-based methods, exploration and exploitation, temporal-difference learning, policy gradients, and stable policy optimization.

The project also serves as a foundation for understanding more advanced methods used in RL for language models and autonomous agents.

## Technologies

Python, NumPy, PyTorch, reinforcement learning.
