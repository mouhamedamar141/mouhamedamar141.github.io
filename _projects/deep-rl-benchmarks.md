## Overview

This project brings together practical experiments with reinforcement learning environments to study how agents learn policies through interaction.

The work includes exercises based on the Hugging Face Deep Reinforcement Learning Course, including PPO on LunarLander and multi-agent experiments involving SoccerTwos.

## Experiments

- **PPO and LunarLander:** exploring policy learning in a simulated control task.
- **Multi-agent learning:** investigating agent behavior in SoccerTwos.
- **Training and evaluation:** observing learning behavior and the effect of environment feedback.
- **Practical implementation:** connecting algorithmic concepts with executable experiments.

## Research motivation

Benchmark environments provide a controlled way to study policy learning, reward design, exploration, and evaluation. They are useful stepping stones toward more complex research in reinforcement learning and robot learning.

## Technologies

Python, reinforcement learning, PPO, Gymnasium, multi-agent environments.
