## Overview

This project explores the use of vision-language models in a maritime-monitoring scenario. It investigates how visual inputs and language-based reasoning can be combined to support an application that interprets information from a scene.

The project was developed in the context of a Liquid AI hackathon and uses the LFM2-VL model family.

## Main themes

- Vision-language model integration.
- Interpretation of visual information through language-model interfaces.
- Agent-oriented application design.
- Exploration of AI-assisted maritime monitoring workflows.

## Research motivation

Vision-language models offer a way to connect perception with semantic reasoning. Exploring their use in monitoring scenarios helps investigate the opportunities and limitations of multimodal AI systems.

This is an experimental application, not a claim of deployment-ready maritime surveillance.

## Technologies

Python, vision-language models, LFM2-VL, multimodal AI.
