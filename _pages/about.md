I am **Mouhamed Amar**, an AI researcher with a Master's degree (M2) in **Artificial Intelligence and Big Data** from École Supérieure Polytechnique, Cheikh Anta Diop University (UCAD), in Dakar, Senegal. My research interests lie at the intersection of **Reinforcement Learning (RL), Robot Learning, Embodied AI, and the post-training of Large Language Models (LLMs)**.

I am particularly interested in developing intelligent agents that can **learn from interaction, make sequential decisions, reason about their environment, and improve through feedback and experience**. My long-term goal is to contribute to research on learning algorithms and agent architectures that enable more capable, adaptive, and autonomous AI systems. Within this broader direction, I am interested in policy optimization, model-free and model-based reinforcement learning, imitation learning, vision-language-action models, and reinforcement learning for reasoning and language models.

During my Master's research internship at **UMMISCO**, I worked on **SDLC-AI**, a framework for adapting the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) to the specific requirements of AI systems. My work explored how to structure the development, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring of machine learning systems while addressing challenges such as reproducibility, model explainability, and data or model drift. This experience strengthened my interest in building reliable, reproducible, and maintainable AI systems throughout their lifecycle.

Alongside my research, I implement and study machine learning and reinforcement learning methods through hands-on projects. I am particularly interested in understanding algorithms from first principles, reproducing research ideas, and comparing approaches through controlled experiments. My current learning and experimentation span policy-gradient methods, modern RL algorithms, behavioral cloning, and robot-learning approaches such as Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT) and Diffusion Policy, as well as LLM post-training methods including RLHF, RLVR, and preference optimization.

I am currently seeking **PhD opportunities in Reinforcement Learning**, particularly in research environments that encourage rigorous experimentation and interdisciplinary collaboration across RL, robotics, and embodied intelligence. Working with limited local computing resources has also encouraged me to prioritize efficient experimentation, open-source tools, and reproducible research workflows, using platforms such as Google Colab and remote computing when available.

Beyond research, I enjoy sharing technical knowledge and making complex concepts more accessible. I am developing educational content in French on **Reinforcement Learning, policy-gradient methods, and LLM post-training**, combining mathematical explanations, intuitive examples, and practical implementations. You can explore my [interactive courses]({{ '/cours/' | relative_url }}) and [research projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}).

I welcome opportunities to collaborate, discuss research ideas, and connect with researchers working on reinforcement learning, robot learning, and the next generation of intelligent agents.
