I am **Mouhamed Amar**, an AI researcher with a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence and Big Data from École Supérieure Polytechnique, Cheikh Anta Diop University (UCAD), Senegal.

My research interests focus on **Reinforcement Learning, Robot Learning, Embodied AI, and LLM post-training**. I am particularly interested in intelligent agents that learn through interaction, make sequential decisions, and improve through feedback and experience.

During my research internship at **UMMISCO**, I worked on SDLC-AI, a framework for adapting the software development lifecycle to AI systems, with a focus on reproducibility, explainability, and model monitoring.

Through hands-on projects, I explore RL algorithms, policy optimization, imitation learning, robot learning, and LLM alignment methods such as RLHF and RLVR.

I am currently seeking **PhD opportunities in Reinforcement Learning**, especially at the intersection of robotics, embodied intelligence, and learning-based agents.

I also enjoy sharing technical knowledge through educational content and practical projects. Explore my [research projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and [blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}).

I welcome research collaborations and discussions on reinforcement learning, robotics, and intelligent agents.
